# PLAYBOOK — "Dental Next 100" Prospect Research Run

## 0. Mission & business context

The operator sells premium website redesigns at $5,000–$15,000 per project. Frontend-focused only: marketing/lead-gen sites, CMS, forms, links to the practice's EXISTING patient portal/booking (NexHealth, ZocDoc, Demandforce, etc. stay third-party). No ecommerce, no portals, no backend builds.

Sales model: build a premium homepage demo from the practice's real information and photography → show the owner-dentist → close the redesign. Therefore every prospect must be one where (a) a demo would be dramatic, (b) the owner can say yes alone or nearly alone, and (c) the practice can obviously afford it.

This run produces the NEXT 100 dental prospects. Two prior rounds already delivered 120 profiled prospects and screened ~380 practices in total — ALL of them are in EXCLUSIONS.md and are off-limits. The 100 findings of this run must be entirely new names.

## 1. The ideal prospect (qualification bar)

An independent, owner-dentist practice that is:
- Established 20+ years (multi-generational is better), OR a strong solo cosmetic/prosthodontic/implant practice with elite credentials (AACD accreditation/fellowship, board-certified prosthodontist, ABOI/ICOI diplomate, Pankey/Dawson/LVI training).
- 2+ doctors preferred (bigger budgets, more at stake); strong solos qualify on credentials.
- Carrying real reputation: review counts, ratings, awards, tenure — verifiable.
- Selling high-ticket elective work: veneers, implants, All-on-4/full-arch, full-mouth reconstruction, Invisalign, sedation.
- In a market that can pay: affluent suburbs and wealthy small cities first, but any US market qualifies if the practice's own scale is proven.
- AND whose website is materially weaker than the business — verified by direct inspection.

What counts as a weak site (all previously observed at scale — look for these exact patterns):
- Dental template vendors, identified from raw HTML footer credits / generator tags / asset paths: ProSites, TNT Dental, PBHS, Officite, Dentalfone, WEO Media (.asp URLs), Sesame 24-7, Great Dental Websites (numeric URL suffixes), Doctor Genius (Materialize CSS), Einstein Dental, TeleVox/Milestone, DentalQore, Internet Dental Alliance (doorway-domain farms), Practice Cafe, DentalMarketing.com (.php), Synergy, iMatrix, GrowthPlug, Thryv/Duda resells, plus DIY Wix/Weebly/Squarespace/GoDaddy builds.
- Signature defects worth quoting verbatim in the profile: frozen copyright years (©2013–©2022), mojibake "Copyright � 2019" footers, EMPTY homepage <title> tags, literal placeholder titles ("Your page title"), phone numbers or ZIP codes inside title tags, keyword-spam titles ("Dentist Near Me | Cost of Dental Care"), dead Google+ links, plain-http "Not Secure" pages, 500-erroring team pages, two competing live domains for one practice, indexed backup pages (/home-bak.html), stock photography with visible watermarks, unsupported WordPress cores (5.x), review corpora of 100–2,000+ invisible on the practice's own homepage.

Disqualify on sight: already-modern/custom premium sites (even for old practices — "old but effective" is not a prospect); any DSO/corporate affiliation; oral surgeons/periodontists as standalone referral practices (out of ICP; prosthodontists ARE in ICP); practices with <~4.0 reputation and unmanaged complaints; obvious retirement-horizon solos with no successor (flag, don't profile, unless credentials are elite).

## 2. DSO / independence screening (mandatory, every finalist)

Corporate groups hide behind legacy local branding. Check ALL of: About-page ownership language; footer entity names; "a division of / an affiliate of" phrasing; booking links pointing to a group domain; WordPress theme names (previous catches: "marquee-dental" → Marquee Dental Partners; "imagen-practice" → Imagen); blog author slugs (catch: "mb2dentaladmin" → MB2); brand patterns ("Dentists of …", "… Modern Dentistry" → Pacific Dental Services). The blocklist is EXCLUSIONS.md §7. If ownership cannot be confirmed, the profile carries "Independence: MEDIUM confidence — verify on call" and loses points; two MEDIUMs max in the final 100's top 50.

Main session after fan-out: dedupe (against EXCLUSIONS.md AND across regions), rank all 108 by score with conversion likelihood as tiebreak, cut to 100, keep 8 bench, run the QC pass (§6), then write outputs (§7).

## 4. Scoring (identical to prior rounds — do not change weights)

100-point opportunity score:
- Financial Capacity 20 · Business Maturity 15 · Website Weakness 15 · Business-vs-Website Gap 15 · Customer Website Dependence 10 · Design Transformation Potential 10 · Conversion Opportunity 5 · Existing Digital Spending 3 · Decision-Maker Accessibility 2

Plus four /10 sub-scores reported separately: Gap, Dependence, Transformation, Technical Simplicity.
Plus conversion likelihood: VERY HIGH / HIGH / MEDIUM-HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW — evidence-based (a bad website alone ≠ likely buyer; existing vendor subscriptions to redirect, named reachable owners, one-screenshot defects, big wasted review corpora, and timing events — anniversaries, generational handoffs — raise it).
Calibration from prior rounds: the top of the range was 88; the cut line for a top-100 fell at 68. Score honestly against that scale.

## 6. QC before finalizing
- Liveness-check all 100 URLs (curl -sk -L, browser UA; 403 bot-walls with verified content count as live — note them).
- Zero overlap with EXCLUSIONS.md — grep the final list against it (names AND domains).
- Zero duplicates across regions.
- Confirm every profile has: a named decision-maker or an explicit UNKNOWN, an independence status, at least one quotable observed defect, and evidence tags.
- Replace failures from each region's candidate table; re-rank.

## 7. Outputs (write all to ./output/)
